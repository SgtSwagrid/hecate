object GroupApi

Descriptions of the endpoints for user groups, which nest through Group.parentId, and whose managers are shared through SharingApi as any resource's holders are. Nobody joins a group without consenting: anyone who may change it joins at once, and anyone else by accepting an invitation, having a request admitted, or following an invite link.

object SharingApi

Endpoints for sharing a resource and its invite link, alike for every kind of resource. Each needs Own on it. A resource the caller may not see, or a principal they may not address, is reported as not found; access may be withdrawn from anyone, such as someone who followed the invite link.
